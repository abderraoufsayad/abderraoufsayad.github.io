Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Ifology is a personal tool used only by the owner of the Ifology YouTube channel to see how the channel's own videos perform.

What it accesses

With the channel owner's permission, it reads the channel's own YouTube data and analytics (video list, views, watch time, retention) through Google's read-only YouTube scopes. It cannot upload, edit or delete anything.

How the data is used and stored

The data is stored only on the owner's own computer and used only to show the owner how the channel's videos are doing. It is not sold, shared, or sent to anyone else.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from your Google Account under Third-party apps & services.

Contact

Through the channel's About page on YouTube.
